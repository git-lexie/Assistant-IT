Assistant XIE – User Manual

Welcome! XIE is your friendly IT-support assistant. You can ask it about computer, network, printer, email, account, security and other work problems, in your own language, by typing or speaking. It works without internet and keeps everything on your own device.

You do not need any IT knowledge. Describe the problem like you would to a colleague, and XIE guides you one step at a time.

1. Getting started

  1. Open the website you were given (for example in Chrome, Edge or Safari).
  2. To use it like an app, tap Install (a small icon in the address bar, or Add to Home screen on a phone). After the first load it also works offline.
  3. XIE says hello. In Settings you can add your name, your email address and your language.

The bottom bar (left side on large screens) has five tabs:

TabWhat it is for
💬 HelpTalk to XIE and get step-by-step fixes
🎫 TicketsYour list of problems, progress and history
📊 ReportsDaily, weekly and monthly reports you can save as PDF
📚 KnowledgeFixes that worked for your company, backups
⚙️ SettingsLook, language, voice, security, backup

2. Asking for help (Help tab)

  1. Choose a category (Wi-Fi, Printer, Email…). XIE shows the common problems in that category: tap the one that matches. Or type your problem in the box, for example "My printer says offline but it is turned on." When a category is selected, XIE only looks inside it (tap ✕ Clear category to search everything). If you describe a different kind of problem, XIE tells you and shows the right guide.
  2. To speak, tap 🎤, talk, and tap Send (or turn on Send automatically after I speak in Settings).
  3. XIE answers with the likely problem, the probable cause, what you need, numbered steps, and when to call IT.
  4. Follow one step at a time. Tell XIE what happens, or ask again with more detail.
  5. Tap 👍 if the answer helped, or 👎 if not. XIE learns from this.

Under an answer you may see Similar solved before. These are fixes you or your team saved earlier. Try them first.

If XIE does not understand, it asks for more detail: which device or app, the exact error message, when it started, and who is affected.

Simple words or Technician

In Settings → How should answers sound?

  • Simple words: plain language, a few easy steps, no jargon. Advanced steps are hidden and XIE tells you when an IT technician is needed.
  • Technician: every step, commands, and root-cause notes.

Feeling tired or stressed?

XIE is also friendly company at work. Tell it how you feel, or tap a mood button (😄 😩 😰 😢 😤).

  • 🫶 Break starts a one-minute guided breathing exercise.
  • ✨ Boost me gives a short pep talk.
  • You can also ask for a joke or a fun fact.

XIE is an AI assistant, not a person. It is happy to chat, and it also hopes you have people around you. If you ever tell XIE you are in a very hard moment, it will point you to emergency services and to the trusted contact you saved in Settings → Trusted contact or local helpline. Please add one.

3. Tickets

Every real problem you describe becomes a ticket.

StatusMeaning
🔴 OpenNot fixed yet
🟠 In progressYou are working on it, or it is delayed
🟢 DoneFixed

To create a ticket yourself: Tickets → + New ticket, describe the issue, pick a category (or Auto-detect), then Create ticket.

Delayed? Mark it In progress. Tap ⏳ In progress, write the reason for the delay, and pick the date and time you will fix it. Overdue items turn red and a banner reminds you. Use 📅 Update schedule to change the date.

When it is fixed: tap Done and write what fixed it (one step per line). XIE saves this and uses it for the next similar problem, so it gets smarter with your team's real fixes.

You can search tickets, filter by status, reopen a ticket, ask XIE about it again, or delete it.

4. Reports

  1. Open Reports and choose Daily, Weekly or Monthly, and pick a date.
  2. The report shows colour-coded totals, a Ticket Status bar chart, a chart by category, and tables of resolved, in-progress (with reason and schedule) and still-open tickets, with signature lines.
  • Daily report: one set of four bars for the day.
  • Weekly report: one group of bars per day, Monday to Sunday (with the date under each day).
  • Monthly report: one group of bars per week of the month (for example 1–4, 5–11, 12–18…).
  • Opened: tickets created in that day, week or month.
  • Still open: tickets opened in that period that stayed open for 30 minutes or more. A brand-new ticket is not counted yet.
  • In progress: tickets opened in that period that you marked ⏳ In progress.
  • Resolved: tickets finished in that period.
  • The bars always add up to the totals at the top. Unresolved tickets from before the period are mentioned in a note under the chart.
  1. Tap Save as PDF and choose Save as PDF as the printer. Colours print by default; if your browser shows Background graphics, leave it on.

5. Knowledge

  • Add a fix: fill in the table (category, problem, cause, things needed, steps). XIE will use it.
  • What XIE has learned: counts of solved tickets, saved fixes and learned words.
  • Load sample tickets: opens a small form. Choose a category (or all 202 guides) and how far back to spread the dates (7 or 30 days), then Add to Tickets. They appear as finished tickets so you can try the reports. Each can be loaded only once.
  • Share / Import knowledge: send your fixes to a colleague as a file, or import theirs.
  • Download backup / Restore from backup: see section 7.

Languages and translation

  • English, Spanish, Filipino, French, German, Portuguese and Chinese are fully built in.
  • About 25 more languages use your browser's on-device translator (Chrome or Edge). Open Settings → Language, then tap Download language pack once. After that it works offline.
  • If that is not available, XIE can use a free online translation service for the app's fixed texts. Nothing you type or say is sent unless you switch on Also translate what I type or say.
  • Speaking and listening follow the chosen language. Your device needs a voice and speech pack for it.

7. Backup, recovery and updates

  • Your data lives only on your device. If you change phone or computer, download a backup first (Knowledge → Download backup), then restore it on the new device.
  • Erase all data and Update both make a backup first: the file downloads, and your email app (or the phone share sheet) opens addressed to your own email. A website cannot send email by itself, so press Send in your email app.
  • New version available: a popup appears. Tap Update now. XIE saves the backup, then updates. Your tickets and settings stay. Tap Later to update another time.
  • Activation code (optional): Settings → Security. The app asks for it every time. The 👁 icon shows or hides what you type. Choose when it locks automatically.
  • Forgot the code? On the lock screen tap Forgot code, type your registered email, and XIE downloads an encrypted backup, opens an email to you, and resets the app. Open the backup later with your recovery key (created automatically once you have a code and an email; save it when it is shown). No saved email means no backup can be sent, so add your email first.

8. Privacy

  • Tickets, fixes, chat and settings are stored only in your browser on your device.
  • XIE works without internet and without any account.
  • Online features are optional: search the web, Wikipedia hints for unknown topics, and free online translation of fixed app texts.
  • Use your device's screen lock too. The activation code keeps others out of the app but does not encrypt browser storage.

10. Quick reference

  1. Describe the problem → follow the steps → 👍 or 👎.
  2. Delayed? ⏳ In progress + reason + date.
  3. Fixed? Done + what fixed it.
  4. Reports → Save as PDF.
  5. Back up before changing devices.
  6. Feeling low or tired? Tell XIE, take a 🫶 Break, and talk to someone you trust.
